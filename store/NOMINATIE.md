- **Description**:
  Crimson Ledger is a murder-mystery logic puzzle made in the Netherlands, in Dutch and English. Each case
  is a floor plan with suspects, rooms and furniture. Players place every suspect from their statements and
  unmask the one person who was alone with the victim. Every case is generated and checked by our own
  engine: exactly one solution, reachable by pure logic, never by guessing. Hints teach the next deduction
  instead of giving the answer away, and a guided practice case teaches the rules in a minute. Version 1.1
  adds a daily case with a streak that unlocks gift worlds at 7 and 30 days. No ads; the first 96 cases are
  free.
- **Notes**: Independent app by a single developer in the Netherlands. Version 1.1 is live since 3 October
  2026; 1.1.1 (store language fix) follows. Strongest in the Netherlands and Belgium (Dutch), also fully
